### 契约 1.1 改了什么（版本号：`CONTRACT_VERSION = '1.1'`，`@weiban/contracts` 1.1.0）

- 新增：用途 `safety_followup`、`behavior_planning`；`SAFETY_OVERDRAFT_PURPOSES`；`GenerateTextInput.safetyPriority` / `countAsBackground` / `meta.conversationKind`；`ReserveInput.safetyOverdraft` / `countAsBackground`；`ReserveOutput.usedSafetyOverdraft`；`ReleaseOutput`；`AdminLedgerEntry.safetyOverdraft`；管理后台用量接口 `ModelAccessAdminUsageEndpoints`（`POST /api/v1/admin/model/usage/summary`、`/records`、`/export`）及相关 schema。
